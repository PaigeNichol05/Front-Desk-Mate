FRONT DESK MATE — AUTHENTICATED FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION

1. Extract this entire ZIP to a folder on your Mac or Windows computer.
2. Install Node.js 24 or newer from https://nodejs.org/ if it is not installed.
3. On Mac, open Start-Front-Desk-Mate.command.
   On Windows, open Start-Front-Desk-Mate.bat.
   The browser opens automatically. If it does not, copy the local address
   printed in the launcher window into your browser.
4. Keep the launcher window open. Ctrl+C stops the app.

Try the updated billing workflow
  As biller, open Billing -> Billing codes & insurance coverage. Search the
  official CMS HCPCS or ICD-10-CM catalog by code/description and service date.
  CPT is not bundled; private licensed import requires an organization ADMIN
  (there is no seeded ADMIN account). Catalog lookup does not verify coverage.
  As physician, document services/medication facts before signing under
  "What happened at this appointment?" Link each service to a source note.
  After signing, review date-valid codes and create a claim from selected
  reviewed performed-service lines. As biller, open the claim and choose a
  fictional paid, rejected or denied outcome. No insurer is contacted.
  Follow docs/DEMO_WALKTHROUGH.md for the full sequence and limitations.

Try the completed audio workflow BEFORE signing the sample encounter
  Sign in as physician. Choose Patients, Open the encounter, then create
  a fictional audio attempt and agree as the physician.
  Sign out. Sign in as patient, open the encounter and agree separately.
  Return as physician, refresh the status, start and finish the synthetic
  attempt, replay the tone, approve it, then release it to the portal.
  Sign in as patient and open Files to replay/download or withdraw consent.

Files and sample metadata persist in this extracted folder across restarts.
The launcher binds only to your own computer (127.0.0.1), chooses a free port,
and does not require editing configuration or installing npm dependencies.
The private data/ and uploads/ folders contain your local demo state.
Do not share those folders. Downloaded copies cannot be recalled on withdrawal.

This is a runnable development demonstration, not a clinical production EHR.
No real patient information is permitted. Audio is a generated tone: no
microphone capture, actual visit recordings, or external transcription.
CMS HCPCS/ICD-10-CM lookup, source-linked coding, detailed toxin records,
staff-entered coverage evidence and a persistent test-only claim outbox are
implemented. Coverage starts unverified; no authenticated member eligibility
response is available. Simulated responses never post real patient payments.
Claims are never sent to insurers. Real clinical/payer use requires the work
documented in docs/PRODUCTION_READINESS.md and docs/INTEGRATIONS.md.

The public Railway concept preview is a separate browser-only walkthrough.
It has no login, database or official code lookup. This package includes the
authenticated server-backed application; the launcher enables synthetic audio
and fictional clearinghouse processing for the bundled sample patient only.
Saved signed/checked-out encounters stay locked after restart. Use a separate
fresh project copy for an independent walkthrough, with fictional data only.
Read README.md and docs/API.md for the implemented features and boundaries.
