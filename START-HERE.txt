FRONT DESK MATE — COMPLETED FICTIONAL DEMONSTRATION

1. Extract this entire ZIP to a folder on your Mac or Windows computer.
2. Install Node.js 24 or newer from https://nodejs.org/ if it is not installed.
3. On Mac, open Start-Front-Desk-Mate.command.
   On Windows, open Start-Front-Desk-Mate.bat.
   The browser opens automatically. If it does not, copy the local address
   printed in the launcher window into your browser.
4. Keep the launcher window open. Ctrl+C stops the app.

Files and sample metadata persist in this extracted folder across restarts.
The launcher binds only to your own computer (127.0.0.1), chooses a free port,
and does not require editing configuration or installing npm dependencies.
The private data/ and uploads/ folders contain your local demo state.
Do not share those folders. Downloaded copies cannot be recalled on withdrawal.

This is a runnable development demonstration, not a clinical production EHR.
No real patient information is permitted. Audio is a generated tone: no
microphone capture, actual visit recordings, or external transcription.
Billing codes are manual specimens and claims are never sent to insurers.
Real clinical/payer use requires the work documented in PRODUCTION_READINESS.md.
